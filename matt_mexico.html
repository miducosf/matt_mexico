01Art / Design · Nuevo PolancoMuseo JumexA love letter to proportion, stone, and light.

One of my favorite museum buildings in the city. David Chipperfield’s pale stone, thoughtful proportions, and beautifully controlled light are as rewarding as the art. Directly across the plaza is the silvery Museo Soumaya—spectacular outside, though its collection felt to me a bit like a wealthy family’s art garage. You may disagree! 😉

For your eye The sawtooth roof, shaded ground floor, and the architectural conversation across the plaza.

02Art / Design · ChapultepecMuseo TamayoA beautiful building and an even better approach.

I love how the building seems to grow out of the park. The layered concrete forms, the open forecourt, and the spaces around it are quietly extraordinary. I would go even just for the architecture and grounds.

For your eye The broad paved courtyard, low horizontal lines, and the building meeting the trees.

Details / website ↗Directions ↗
03Art / Design · ChapultepecMuseo Nacional de AntropologíaBig ideas, astonishing objects, extraordinary space.

A magnificent museum of Mexico’s Indigenous cultures and history—and a remarkable architectural experience in its own right. Its vast courtyard and dramatic umbrella fountain are unforgettable. No need to see everything in one visit.

For your eye The canopy reflected in wet stone and the interplay of pattern, scale, and shadow.

04Art / Design · Roma NorteGalería OMRContemporary art in a compelling setting.

One of the galleries I’d put high on your list. The exhibition rooms and architecture have a presence of their own, and the changing work always makes it interesting. A wonderful excuse to wander Roma.

For your eye How the gallery’s surfaces and openings frame the work.

05Food · Hipódromo / CondesaMendl DelicatessenBagels, then a lovely surprise out back.

Delicious bagels and a beautiful leafy patio tucked behind the deli. I think you’ll love the light and tropical-modernist feeling back there as much as the food. Ask to sit in the rear courtyard if you can.

For your eye Filtered light, greenery, and that marvelous indoor–outdoor feeling.

06Food · Centro HistóricoCafé El PopularThe delicious bustle of an old-school institution.

No curated minimalism here! This is a proper, busy Mexico City institution: comforting food, coffee, people coming and going, and a wonderful sense of everyday life. I love it for precisely that.

For your eye The human theater of a busy café.

07Food · Centro HistóricoChurrería El MoroHot churros, hot chocolate. Enough said.

NOTE: There's many locations. My favorite is in Condesa next to the Parque Mexico. You can walk off the calories in the park! ;) An institution for good reason. Go for freshly made churros and a rich hot chocolate. It is a perfect little indulgence between adventures or at the end of a full day exploring.

For your eye Its iconic blue-and-white details and the choreography behind the counter.

Details / website ↗Directions ↗
08Cozy · Roma NorteCasa Guillermo Tovar de TeresaAn intimate house of history, objects, and atmosphere.

A lovely historic townhouse filled with traditional interiors, collected objects, and beautiful old-world details. A calmer, more intimate counterpoint to the monumental museums. Here is the twist: it is actually another Museo Soumaya location!

For your eye Framed views through doorways, furniture, decorative details, and the quiet garden.
